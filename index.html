Why facilities choose us

How it works

From call to proof of delivery

Four steps on every run, whether it is a STAT dispatch or a standing daily route.

  1. 1

    Schedule a pickup

    Call dispatch or send the two addresses. You get a window quoted for that specific run.

  2. 2

    Courier dispatched

    One named courier is assigned. Nothing else rides with your specimen.

  3. 3

    Secure transport

    Sealed and logged at pickup, held at temperature, driven direct with no route deviation.

  4. 4

    Proof of delivery

    Signed and timestamped at the receiving department. The closed manifest is yours on request.

Compliance and regulations

The rules this work runs under

  • HIPAA Privacy & Security

    Protected health information in transit: a signed BAA, safeguards, and breach notification that runs back to your facility.

  • OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens

    Written exposure control plan, annual training, protective equipment, and a defined spill and exposure procedure.

  • DOT Category B (UN 3373)

    Triple packaging for diagnostic specimens: leakproof primary and secondary, absorbent, and a marked rigid outer container.

  • CLIA Specimen Integrity

    Your lab sets acceptance and rejection criteria. A broken temperature range or an exceeded stability window is a rejection.

Common questions

What labs ask before they switch

If your question is not here, call dispatch and ask.

How fast can you pick up a STAT run?

Response windows are quoted per address pair rather than published as one number, because a Midtown to White Plains run is not the same job as a run between two Brooklyn addresses. Call dispatch with both addresses for a window you can hold us to.

Will you sign our business associate agreement?

Yes. A courier handling protected health information is generally a business associate under HIPAA, so we sign your BAA and train every courier on PHI in transit annually.

Can you issue a certificate of insurance to our facility?

The certificate of insurance is issued directly to your facility, named to your entity, and a renewal copy is sent before it lapses.

Do you run north of the city, into Westchester and beyond?

Yes. Westchester, Rockland and Putnam Counties are standard coverage, not a special request. Coverage spans New York City, Nassau and Suffolk Counties on Long Island, and those three Hudson Valley counties.

What temperature ranges can you carry?

Ambient and refrigerated 2 to 8 degrees Celsius are standard. Frozen and dry-ice transport are available on confirmation. The reading is logged for the run and reported with the manifest.

Do you offer scheduled daily routes as well as on-call runs?

Yes. Recurring pickups run in a fixed daily window on the days you choose, with the same courier on your route wherever possible.
